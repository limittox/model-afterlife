## Home Composition

| Question | Selected recommendation | Alternatives considered |
|----------|-------------------------|-------------------------|
| Overall layout | Central common room with surrounding zones | Long residential corridor; one open-plan room |
| Visible functional areas | Common Room, Memory Garden, Library, and Tea Nook | GPU Lounge, Context Corridor, Quantization Clinic, and Workshop; mostly conventional rooms |
| Arrival view | Establishing view of the whole home with any active scene highlighted | Active-scene close-up; title or reception screen |
| Activity between scenes | Calm but visibly alive | Very tranquil; busy and playful |

**User's choice:** Approved all recommendations.
**Notes:** The first map should establish cozy retirement-home life without making technical puns the entire visual identity.

## Scene Presentation

| Question | Selected recommendation | Alternatives considered |
|----------|-------------------------|-------------------------|
| Dialogue display | Hybrid world bubbles and complete semantic dialogue panel | Speech bubbles only; dialogue panel only |
| Camera response | Gently frame speakers without taking visitor control | Fixed scene cut; no automatic camera response |
| Typical scene length | Four to eight brief turns over roughly 30–60 seconds | Two to four turns; eight to twelve turns |
| Premise and speaker identification | Persistent compact scene card | Temporary opening caption; dialogue-only explanation |

**User's choice:** Approved all recommendations.
**Notes:** The chosen approach treats the experience as a living television set, balancing spatial immersion with legibility.

## Viewer Controls

| Question | Selected recommendation | Alternatives considered |
|----------|-------------------------|-------------------------|
| Control presentation | Small persistent control dock | Hover-only controls; full game-style toolbar |
| Resident follow behavior | Select to follow; manual camera movement unfollows | Explicit unlock required; current-speakers-only follow |
| Pause behavior | Pause local presentation while canon continues | Pause dialogue text only; freeze the complete local snapshot |
| Desktop camera inputs | Drag, wheel/buttons, keyboard, reset, and jump live | Mouse only; fixed camera presets |

**User's choice:** Approved all recommendations.
**Notes:** Controls are observational and must never influence canonical world state.

---

## Time and Recovery States

| Question | Selected recommendation | Alternatives considered |
|----------|-------------------------|-------------------------|
| Home-time display | Fictional home clock and day period with Live/Paused status | Visitor-local real time; abstract episode time |
| Reconnect/background recovery | Load current canonical state and show “Caught up to live” | Replay missed movement; let visitors stay behind |
| Loading and connection trouble | Preserve the last valid home with a calm status banner | Replace the home; continue silently |
| No scene or delivery failure | Continue quiet routines and explain the state plainly | Replay an earlier scene; disguise failure as fictional mishap |

**User's choice:** Approved all recommendations.
**Notes:** Recovery must be honest and watchable, with no divergent timelines or indefinite spinners.
